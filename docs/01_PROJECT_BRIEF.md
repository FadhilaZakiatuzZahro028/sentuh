# 01 — Project Brief

## Product
**Sentuh** (working brand, trademark/domain availability **not yet checked**) is a physical acrylic QR + NFC stand that opens a configurable public profile page for a business or institution. The product may serve cafes, shops, schools, clinics and other organizations without separate website builds.

**Value proposition:** one scan or tap opens the owner's selected information and actions: contact, maps, website, menus, enrollment, social links and optional Google Review. Review remains on Google's platform; Sentuh does not generate ratings.

## Course constraints
- Course: Entrepreneurship final assignment.
- Deadline: **1 November 2026**; target internal finish **28 October 2026**.
- Team: **2 people**. Member 1 owns coding/design; member 2 owns research, proposal, report and business documentation. Product and demo testing are shared.
- Budget: **under Rp100,000** for the initial prototype. No paid infrastructure before evaluating the need.
- Required: physical product, website/demo, sales exercise (fictional customer permitted by instructor), business proposal, report and presentation.
- Clearly label any fictional customer, sales number, testimonial or demonstration data as simulated.

## MVP deliverables
1. Live landing page introducing Sentuh and offering inquiry via WhatsApp.
2. Protected internal dashboard to create and edit organizations, links and physical devices.
3. One responsive reusable page per organization, with editable logo, description, theme and configurable buttons.
4. Stable device-specific QR and NFC URLs pointing to the correct business page.
5. At least one working acrylic prototype; test on real phones and demonstrate Google Review/map/contact links with *permitted* sample destinations.
6. Landing-page guided FAQ chat widget with optional WhatsApp handoff if the core is complete.
7. Screenshots, tests, costs and transparent simulated-sales evidence for the academic report.

## Not in MVP
Customer self-serve login, subscription billing, payment gateway, generative AI API chatbot, Google Business Profile API, reviews scraped from Google, unlimited page-layout editor, complex analytics, custom domains for each customer.

## Success criteria
- From admin login, create three distinct demo organization types without modifying code.
- Generate device-specific QR, scan, and open the correct published page on a phone.
- Program an NFC tag with a device URL and successfully tap a compatible phone.
- Configure a business link and verify destination; a missing link is not displayed.
- Provide documented demo and business deliverables by the course deadline.

## Risks and mitigation
- Free hosting can sleep or pause: warm up before demo, keep screenshots/video backup; no promise of 24/7 commercial uptime.
- Printed QR codes are hard to change: use a stable project domain before actual sales; demo-only printed URLs must be identified as prototype URLs.
- Budget may vary by shipping: buy **one** acrylic and a few NFC tags after template dimensions are final.
- Scope creep: ship main QR-to-page flow first; FAQ chat and analytics come later.
